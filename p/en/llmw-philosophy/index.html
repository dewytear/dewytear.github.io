Use and Philosophy — Reading Ability and Running Two Vaults

The wiki is a storage space for ingredients

The LLM wiki is a storage space for ingredients. The dishes cooked from them (the outputs) — curricula, lecture plans, and so on — move over to the main vault. If an output is too large to serve comfortably, it is managed in a separate dev (development) folder. (Heavy files such as HTML and node_modules are managed outside the vault, to prevent rendering slowdowns.)

The most important ability — reading

In the age of AI, the most important ability is the ability to read. It is easy to set an agent to work and then not read, but you can only direct it properly if you read it yourself. The instructor places the very purpose of setting up AI in "securing time for me to read". The wiki's concept, entity, guide, and query cards are all composed to be easy for people to read too.

Why split into two vaults

Stating the author and the .claude rules differ between the main vault (which I manage) and the LLM vault (which the AI handles on its own). Merging them into one brings more errors and contaminated information, so keeping them separate is better. If you want to move part of an existing vault into the wiki, you can migrate by starting the agent in the starter kit vault, giving it the address of the existing vault, and saying "move over what is worth making into a wiki".

Wrapping up — stay attached to AI knowledge directly

Leaders divide into two kinds — "tell me more" and "a member can just do it, why should I learn?". But the knowledge ceiling of my member (partner) becomes my own ceiling. That is why we have to attach ourselves directly to AI knowledge, and for that we need our own knowledge repository. Obsidian is the best tool for it.

Q&A highlights · Shared vaults — official Sync, or self-hosting with osync (NAS, cloud, Docker). Even simultaneous editing is generally safe within 1 to 2 seconds.
· Scraping PDFs — convert if consistency matters; if you only need the context, turning them into markdown with Python saves tokens.
· Metadata fields are decided by the person who will retrieve the knowledge — design the key-values to match the classification you will retrieve by.
· Vault scale — filter by metadata (type and the like) and even ten thousand notes can be indexed and accessed without trouble.
