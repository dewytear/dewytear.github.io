LLM Wiki — Overview

This material organizes the "LLM Wiki / Yohan Gu" talk paragraph by paragraph, in order. In the age of artificial intelligence, where and how you organize your own material matters enormously. We have already been extending context to AI with markdown files such as Claude Code's CLAUDE.md and memory.md, and Obsidian is a system that contextualizes all of it into a single framework.

Without heavy RAG or recompilation, AI follows the vault's map (signposts) to reach knowledge live.

What LLM Wiki is

It is a concept proposed by Karpathy in an April 2026 X post ("LLM Knowledge Bases") and the 'llm-wiki' gist: a system that lets artificial intelligence reference knowledge by embedding live, without heavy RAG that builds vector or graph databases and without continuous compiling. Once you build a knowledge base in an Obsidian vault, AI looks at the map and navigation (signposts) and finds its way to the knowledge.

What this document set covers

  • The vault system — main vault vs. wiki vault
  • What goes in (knowledge capture) and note metadata
  • Note links and the kinds of wiki cards
  • System files and rules, plus hands-on onboarding
  • The three core operations — Ingest · Query · Lint
  • The web clipper and inbox, and the philosophy of putting it to use
