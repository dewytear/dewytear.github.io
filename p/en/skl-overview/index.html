Skill Deep Dive — Overview

This material organizes the "Skill Deep Dive / Taein Kim" course paragraph by paragraph, in order. Learning how to use skills takes all of five minutes — it is that easy. So this course focuses less on usage and more on the concept of a skill and the philosophy and ideas that drive it.

Organize recurring know-how into a skill once, and new tasks, other projects, and your team reuse it as is.
Why learn it deeply Understanding skills properly helps a great deal in grasping not just a single technique but the whole ecosystem in your future work and in applying generative AI. The harness that comes next is, in a way, a kind of meta skill, so having the concept of skills down makes that material much clearer too.

What this document set covers

  • The background from which skills were born — the coding agent
  • The concept of skills and their strengths
  • The context window and the principle of progressive loading
  • The SKILL.md structure and writing the description
  • Example skills, hands-on installation and use, security cautions
  • Building skills (skill engineering) and a summary of the engineering layers
