Scenario Board

We gathered, module by module, the situations in ERP client customization where a harness is worth attaching. Read each card in the order module · difficulty · situation · recommended team · expected effect. Pick a similar situation and try running it by changing only the intent sentence.

Difficulty markers Beginner standalone, small-scale customization · Intermediate affects the standard · Advanced multiple modules, data, consistency risk. The higher the difficulty, the more weight you should give to regression verification and human review.

Auto-holding orders that exceed the credit limit

Sales · ordersIntermediate

Automatically hold orders that go over the credit limit for each credit rating and notify the person in charge. Impact analysis of the standard credit and order logic is essential.

Recommended team
Impact analystCustomization designerDevelopmentRegression QA

Effect — blocks over-the-limit mistakes · standardizes the approval flow

Distribution center location (bin) management add-on

Inventory · logisticsIntermediate

An add-on that puts cell- and location-level receiving and shipping in the client's warehouse on top of standard inventory. Inventory receipt-and-issue consistency is the crux.

Recommended team
Inventory flow analystData designerDevelopmentReceipt-issue consistency QA

Effect — visibility of actual stock per location · fewer mis-shipments

Client-specific allowance · deduction rules

Payroll · HRIntermediate

Reflect allowance and deduction formulas tailored to the client's employment rules in payroll calculation. The effect on the four major insurances and year-end tax settlement has to be checked.

Recommended team
Payroll rule analystFormula designerDevelopmentPayslip consistency QA

Effect — higher accuracy in reflecting the rules↑ · fewer payroll error inquiries

Account mapping · auto-journalizing customization

Accounting · closingAdvanced

Customize the automatic journalizing rules for slips to match the client's chart of accounts. The risk to closing and financial-statement consistency is large.

Recommended team
Chart-of-accounts analystJournalizing rule designerDevelopmentBalance · closing QA

Effect — less manual journalizing · shorter closing lead time

BOM · process routing customization

ProductionAdvanced

Customize BOM explosion and process routing to match the client's production method. Analysis of the chain effects on requirement and cost calculation is needed.

Recommended team
Production flow analystBOM designerDevelopmentCost · requirement QA

Effect — reflects the actual process · smaller plan-vs-actual gap

Adding an electronic approval step for purchase orders

Purchasing · POsBeginner

Add approval steps by amount bracket to the standard purchase order flow. A relatively localized customization.

Recommended team
Approval flow analystDevelopmentRegression QA

Effect — stronger approval control · regulatory compliance

Developing a tailored management report format

ReportsBeginner

Produce a management report with the metrics and layout the client wants out of standard data. Since it only queries standard data, the risk is low.

Recommended team
Metric definition analystReport developmentNumerical consistency QA

Effect — shorter report writing time · metric consistency

Legacy → ERP initial migration

Data migrationAdvanced

Migrate the masters and balances of the old system into the ERP. Since mapping, consistency, and verification are large volumes of repetitive work, splitting the load across a team pays off a lot.

Recommended team
Mapping analystConversion developmentConsistency verificationMigration QA

Effect — migration errors detected in advance · automated reconciliation

Standard package bug analysis · patch

MaintenanceIntermediate

Pin down the cause of a standard defect whose reproduction path is unclear using logs and source, and propose a minimal patch. Grasping the scope of impact is the key.

Recommended team
Reproduction · cause analystPatch designDevelopmentRegression QA

Effect — shorter time to pin down the cause · prevents recurrence

Developing interfaces to external systems

Integration · I/FAdvanced

Develop integration (messages/API) with bank, logistics, and group affiliate systems. The design burden for exceptions, reprocessing, and consistency is large.

Recommended team
Integration spec analystI/F designerDevelopmentException · reprocessing QA

Effect — removes manual integration · automates error reprocessing

Auto-generating regression test scenarios

Quality · QABeginner

Automatically derive the standard and custom scenarios to check after a customization is applied, and organize them into a checklist.

Recommended team
Impact scope analystScenario writingVerification

Effect — prevents missed verification · shorter QA preparation time

CR impact · effort estimation

Analysis · planningBeginner

Estimate in advance the effect a customer change request (CR) has on the standard and on existing customizations along with the expected effort, and reflect it in the quote and schedule.

Recommended team
Requirement analystImpact analystEffort estimation

Effect — prevents under- and over-quoting · early risk identification
