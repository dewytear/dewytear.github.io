Scenarios, Harness 100, Commands

We gathered, module by module, the situations in ERP client customization where a harness is worth attaching. Read each card in the order module · difficulty · situation · recommended team · expected effect. Pick a similar situation and try running it by changing only the intent sentence.

Difficulty markers Beginner standalone, small-scale customization · Intermediate affects the standard · Advanced multiple modules, data, consistency risk. The higher the difficulty, the more weight you should give to regression verification and human review.

Auto-holding orders that exceed the credit limit

Sales · ordersIntermediate

Automatically hold orders that go over the credit limit for each credit rating and notify the person in charge. Impact analysis of the standard credit and order logic is essential.

Recommended team
Impact analystCustomization designerDevelopmentRegression QA

Effect — blocks over-the-limit mistakes · standardizes the approval flow

Distribution center location (bin) management add-on

Inventory · logisticsIntermediate

An add-on that puts cell- and location-level receiving and shipping in the client's warehouse on top of standard inventory. Inventory receipt-and-issue consistency is the crux.

Recommended team
Inventory flow analystData designerDevelopmentReceipt-issue consistency QA

Harness100 · Performance

Harness100 is a published collection that pre-builds harnesses for 100 frequently used cases. Instead of putting a team together from scratch every time, it is far faster to find a similar setup and customize it.

Performance — how much better does it get

15 development tasks (basic · intermediate · advanced) were solved without a harness vs with a harness, and the final outputs were scored by an LLM judge. The result was an average of about 60% performance improvement, with a 100% win rate when a harness was set up. In particular, the harder the task, the more the single-tool score dropped, whereas the harness kept its quality.

Where the numbers come from The "60% improvement · 100% win rate" above is the lecturer's own measurement. For reference, Anthropic has also announced that a multi-agent setup showed a 90.2% performance improvement in internal evaluation compared with a single agent, so the trend itself — "splitting the roles is advantageous on hard tasks" — is consistent.
A conceptual chart — a trend, not actual values. The harder the task, the wider the gap.
Caution The graph above is a conceptual chart showing a trend; for exact numbers, check the published benchmark materials. Because in ERP customization the domain's nature makes standard impact and regression decide the quality, verification against your own field's standards matters more than benchmark numbers.

Commands · Quick Reference

The commands, controls, and settings you look up most often while using the harness, gathered in one place.

Core commands

CommandWhat it does
/plugin marketplace add <repo>Registers the harness marketplace (e.g. lev-factory/harness)
/plugin install harnessInstalls the Harness plugin
/reloadApplies the installed plugin to the current session
/harness <intent>Gives an intent and runs the harness (starts forming the team)
ProceedApproves execution after reviewing the proposed team
/agentsChecks the list of created agents
/exitEnds the session (to re-enter when agents are not showing)

Controls · shortcuts

TabAutocompletes after typing /harness
Shift + arrow keysPeeks into the work of each running agent

A new terminal windowRuns another harness at the same time (parallel work)
