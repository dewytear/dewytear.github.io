What Goes In — Knowledge Capture

"If I just use a recent model with web search, why bother organizing things locally?" is a natural question. The answer lies in the limits of our own heads and in learning.

A forward base for learning — the stretched goal

The stretched goal from educational technology is the idea that learning happens smoothly only when you set a goal you can reach by stretching your arm a little further. The LLM wiki plays the role of this forward base for learning. You capture material you do not understand yet but want to learn, and inch forward with the help of AI.

Let AI know what you care about

Like the links and articles you pile up in KakaoTalk's "send to myself," we capture knowledge we have taken an interest in. Artificial intelligence does not know all of your tastes, but once you let it know where you showed interest and what you applied your human bias to, there is far more it can learn from there.

What goes into the main vault vs. the wiki vault

  • Main vault — concepts you know well enough to lecture on all day.
  • LLM wiki — concepts you want to talk about all day but do not know well yet. Often the more technical material.

For example, overseas cases researched while looking into mounting Starlink on a vehicle, or concepts learned while writing up BM25 (Best Matching 25, lexicon-based search), go into the wiki.

Key point What matters is organizing the concepts you intend to study in a form that not only you can read, but artificial intelligence can read alongside you.
