The Strengths of Skills

Usually a product's characteristics are a list of several features built separately. Yet the strengths of skills all derive from the single idea of the "skill". So once you understand the concept, the characteristics connect naturally.

StrengthWhat it means
ReusabilityBuild it once and productivity rises a lot even if you never share it and just use it many times yourself.
Packaging repetitive workTurn work you always do the same way into a skill and handle it easily and consistently every time.
Progressive loadingLoads only when needed, reducing context waste (the core principle, in a separate document).
Scripts and resources includedLayer in proven existing scripts as they are, making probabilistic results deterministic.
Composite workflowsSeveral skills can be loaded at once and combined (fused).
Platform compatibilityUsable in Claude AI, API, Code, and Cowork (environments and permissions differ a little). Partly usable in Codex and Antigravity as well.
Packaging expertise and organizational knowledgeStandardize by capturing tone and manner, review checklists, quality standards, and the like in a skill.

Proven scripts as they are

Skills turn complaints like "what the AI writes differs every time, so I can't use it" and "there are hallucinations" into deterministic results that run inside the script I provided. If you layer a proven script you have used for years (for example, a field's traditional analysis script) into a skill, you can put it to use reliably without hallucination.

The value it gives an organization It carries great significance that the outputs coming out of our institution can be produced to a set quality standard. Package domain expertise and organizational knowledge into a skill, and no matter who makes it, the result is consistent — "as if one person wrote it".
