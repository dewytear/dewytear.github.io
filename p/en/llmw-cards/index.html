Kinds of Wiki Cards and Raw Sources

A researcher has to state the source of their knowledge. So raw material is held as a raw source, marking where the knowledge came from. (Automated indexing and citation through a connected reference manager such as Zotero or Bookends is also planned.)

Raw source → wiki card

Put articles, news stories, papers, YouTube transcripts, and the like into the vault, and the LLM reads them, extracts from them, and turns them into wiki cards. If there are five things to learn from a single article, five cards can come out of it, and you can also ingest several sources at once (3 articles + 2 YouTube videos) and let the AI compile them on its own.

Raw sources become source-based wiki cards as the LLM reads them — split into concepts, entities, guides, and maps.

Kinds of cards

KindContent
ConceptA basic concept card.
EntityProper nouns, service names, people's names, and so on.
GuideA phrase that contains concepts and entities — a how-to, like "do this with that".
Maps / LabsConcept cards that cover the whole picture.
Capture tip — leave the purpose behind When capturing knowledge, always write the purpose after the # (hash). This is so you can search for it later. Since you end up capturing things and never looking at them again, it is good to leave hints that future-you can reach through search. When you hand off an ingest as well, if you have it ask "why did you collect this / what are you trying to do", it takes that intent and connects and reasons with past research in the main vault.
