AI 2040: Plan A — the “positive scenario” that says delay superintelligence until 2040 (overview)

The AI Futures Project, known for AI 2027, has published its follow-up, “AI 2040: Plan A”. This document is not a prediction of the future but a normative scenario (a recommendation) that says “this is what we would like to see happen.” If AI 2027 painted the ending of the superintelligence race as human extinction or an irreversible concentration of power, Plan A is the alternative the same authors put forward saying “let us do this instead.” This article is not a verbatim rendering of the original but a commentary compiled by cross-checking public summaries, press reports and author comments, and it is the first installment (overview and hub) of the four-part series in this folder.

The gist in one sentence

Instead of running as fast as possible toward the most powerful systems, the US and China should agree to deliberately delay the birth of superintelligence until 2040, make all frontier AI research public (transparent) in the meantime, and build a structure in which they can monitor and verify each other. The authors hold that without intervention superintelligence arrives around 2030, and they say that pushing that moment back ten years until safety infrastructure is in place is “the least bad plan I know of.”

“In AI 2027, we predicted that AI would take over the world or irreversibly concentrate power. In AI 2040: Plan A, we’ve laid out our positive vision for what should happen instead.”

— Daniel Kokotajlo (X, 2026-07-09)

The original’s interactivity — what “moves”

ai-2040.com is not a plain article but an interactive document. The confirmed elements are:

  • Step-by-step, branching narrative — following the scenario in stages and unfolding the other options at each fork, a choices structure (plan-a-root).
  • Modified AI Futures Model explorer — an interactive for adjusting, yourself, the model that produces Plan A’s capability trajectory.
  • Economic growth explorer — modeling the economy of the AI scenario with factors such as capital, robots, prices, land and energy (Supplements).
  • Audio, mobile and desktop editions — the experience is richest on desktop.

Applied to this wiki too? — the clickable timeline above is the answer. The step-by-step interaction has been reproduced self-containedly, without external libraries, in this wiki’s design language (theme tokens, lightweight JS). The full economic model simulator, by contrast, is too large in scope, so it is left as a static summary plus a link to the original.

The boundary between fact and estimate Confirmed facts: the document’s existence, its authors and release date, its character as “a recommendation, not a prediction”, the core substance of delay to 2040, total transparency and mutually assured compute destruction, and the existence of the reactions. Estimated and contested territory: the feasibility of this plan (it presumes US–China trust and state capacity), the assumption that superintelligence arrives in 2030, and the realism of relocating data centers — the authors themselves state that it is “a device for pressing and testing policy, not a prediction.”
