What I'm Exploring

My biggest interest right now is the combination of AI and enterprise software. In particular, I keep exploring these questions.

AI × ERP

Beyond just reading ERP data, can AI…

  • understand the state of the business,
  • interpret business rules,
  • detect anomalies,
  • and suggest or take action at the right moment?

AI × Software Development

Beyond generating code, can AI…

  • understand the existing architecture,
  • trace business rules,
  • analyze the impact of changes,
  • perform code reviews,
  • and accumulate development experience as organizational knowledge?

AI × Knowledge

Every project accumulates…

  • Architecture
  • Business Rule
  • Database Semantics
  • API
  • Decision
  • Work Log

— can these become a knowledge system that is continuously generated, connected, and updated, instead of depending on human memory?

These questions are where my focus is today.

How I Work

These are the principles I value.

01

Structure Before Complexity

The more complex the problem, the less I rush to implement. I first find the structure, split responsibilities, and define boundaries.

02

Knowledge Should Survive People

Knowledge must remain even when people move on. Code, documents, issues, decisions, and incident experience should become organizational assets, not personal memories.

03

Automate Repetition

I automate whatever people repeat. AI should not add new work for people — it should naturally generate knowledge and raise productivity within existing workflows.

04

Build for the Future

I prefer structures that can absorb the next change over implementations that only solve today's requirements — while investing in structure only where real extensibility is likely, rather than over-abstracting.

Why This Wiki Exists

This wiki is not just a document store. It is a knowledge base where I accumulate what I learn while building, thinking, and experimenting — so it stays useful over time.

In one sentence — it is a publicly published LLM wiki written by AI (Claude) and curated by a human, and a second brain that grows through capture → organize → connect → search. Three traits set it apart from an ordinary notebook.

Published, Not Private

Not a personal vault but knowledge published on GitHub Pages — humans and AIs read the same canonical source at the same address.

Dual Interface

One repository serves humans a trilingual UI, unified full-text and concept search, nine graph views plus Graph Studio, and citation backlinks (citedBy) between documents — and serves AIs a machine interface of llms.txt & knowledge-graph.json along with a full-corpus dump, static snapshots (/p/), and feeds. Documents are linked through a four-tier hierarchy (World→Domain→System→Document) and five typed semantic relations: a RAG-ready knowledge source any agent can consume as-is.

Machine Discipline

Editorial rules — trilingual parity, typed relations between documents, creation dates, cache consistency — are enforced by CI gates, not by habit. That is why the shape of the knowledge never drifts.
