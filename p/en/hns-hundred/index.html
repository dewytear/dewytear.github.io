Harness 100

A published collection that composes 100 case harnesses in advance for the things you need in daily life and at work. (Search for "harness 100"; a Korean version is provided.) The fields are as follows.

  • Content production (YouTube planning · podcasts · newsletters · online courses)
  • Software development, data analysis
  • Business strategy (from validating a startup idea to pitching, government support program business plans)
  • Education · learning, law (risk assessment), health · lifestyle, communication, operating processes, specialized domains

For example, "writing a government support program business plan" already comes with agents like a public notice analysis expert · business viability section writer · budget planning helper and the related skills. Users make use of it by finding a similar composition and customizing it.

If a site you want to pull from needs an API integration, you can make that itself into a skill and connect it to an agent.

Measuring performance — putting numbers on it

These are trends, not actual measurements — the harness gap widens as the difficulty rises.

To check the felt effect in numbers, 15 development tasks by difficulty (basic · intermediate · advanced) were compared without a harness vs with one, and the final code was evaluated by an LLM judge.

Result On average about a 60% performance improvement, and a 100% win rate when a harness was composed. Especially the harder the task — using only a single tool made the score drop a lot, but the harness had the effect of keeping the quality.
About the numbers The numbers above are not a public benchmark but the speaker's own measurements. For reference, Anthropic has also announced that a multi-agent configuration showed a 90.2% performance improvement over a single agent in its internal evaluation (multi-agent research system).
