Proposal Overview — A GitLab-Based AI Development Knowledge Platform

Developers keep doing exactly what they do now — landing their work in GitLab — while AI analyzes those results and automatically converts and accumulates them as organizational knowledge. This proposes a Read-Only Engineering Knowledge Platform. It is not a wiki that people write; it is a structure in which the development artifacts themselves are the source of knowledge.

In one sentence A Read-Only Engineering Knowledge Platform in which AI continuously analyzes the development artifacts accumulating in GitLab and converts them into the organization's currently valid development knowledge.

And this is not a blank-sheet design. The knowledge storage, indexing, and linking engine of this proposal is already implemented and proven in the Reference Wiki that runs as an advance validation — as of 2026-07, measured: 107 documents · 337 unique concepts · 349 automatic related edges · 107 human-declared typed knowledge relations, all running on top of a deterministic registration pipeline. The Knowledge Management and Asset Utilization installments of this series cover how that engine scales to company size. In other words, this proposal is not imagining "what could be built" but describing "how to connect a proven engine already in operation to GitLab".

The Final Picture — A Virtuous Cycle

The goal is not the wiki itself. It is a virtuous cycle in which development results become knowledge, and the accumulated knowledge in turn raises the productivity of the next development. A wiki written by people demanded the extra labor of "develop → write a document → register it in the Wiki" and never lasted; in this structure development experience becomes an organizational asset, repeated analysis shrinks, experience is reused across projects, onboarding gets faster, design intent is preserved, and AI tools such as Claude Code use organizational knowledge as Context.

A structure where the organization gets smarter the more it develops — this is the final direction of this proposal.
